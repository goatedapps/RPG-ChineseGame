## Review method

Coverage counts unique vocabulary entries in the story prose, with repeated uses counting once.
Titles and page headings do not contribute to coverage.
The lesson 2 character 言 appearing only inside 语言 and the lesson 14 character name 胖胖 are excluded from the counts.
Single-character words elsewhere are used with their taught meanings, such as 双 for a pair, 根 for a plant root, and 金 for gold.
Each story was reviewed for character continuity, the sequence of events, vocabulary meaning and whether the ending resolves the main problem.
The clock story explicitly separates the dream from waking life.
The whale model’s problem is its long tail preventing a turn in the doorway, so folding the tail resolves it.
The duck story distinguishes an unfinished task from a carelessly finished ending.
The gardening story describes reducing future watering, rather than undoing water already added.
The [coverage record](story-coverage.yaml) lists the used and unused words for every lesson.
